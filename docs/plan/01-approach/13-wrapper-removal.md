### Workstream: Wrapper removal

This phase is one step and one commit. It changes no behaviour.

#### Wrappers step 1. Delete 14 forwarding functions and move their callers
Anchor: David Parnas, Dieter Rams, Tony Hoare

A wrapper stays when its caller has fewer concepts with it than without it. That holds for a platform seam with a sibling under another build tag, for a function that fixes an argument the caller must not know, for a named domain rule, and for the exported entry to an unexported encoder. The 45 one-line functions in `internal/` and `cmd/` were each read with their callers. 31 stay for one of those reasons. These 14 only repeat another call under a second name, or have no caller.

Make these edits. Line numbers are from commit `3c4b6f94`; apply the deletions in one file from the bottom up.
