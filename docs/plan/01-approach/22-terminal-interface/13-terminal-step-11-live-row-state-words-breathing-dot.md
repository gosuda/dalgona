- `dock.go` `func (m *model) liveRow() string`: the live row is the line below the panel and above the status band. It is drawn in every window of 3 rows or more, and the fit of Step 3 drops it in a shorter window. The elapsed field is the only right-aligned field in the row. Every other field is left-aligned in its cells. The fields are fixed and come in this order from the left:
  - dot, 2 cells. While `running`, the dot from `m.screen.spin.View()` and one space. Otherwise 2 blank cells.
  - live tag (cross-phase decision 59). The dot cells and the state word field are the first 10 cells of the row. While `running` and no decision is open, these 10 cells are drawn through `t.liveTag(threadHue(m.thread.id), text)`: the point fill, with the dot and the word in point ink and bold. In every other state they have no fill.
  - state word, 8 cells. The word of `stateWord`, padded to 8 cells, or cut with `fit` when it is longer (all display words have at most 7 cells, so each fits the 8-cell field; cross-phase decision 50). It shows while `running` (`Working`), while `awaiting approval` or `awaiting input` (`Waiting`, plain `dim` with no dot, because the open decision holds the point, Design record, The point and the thread hue), and for `interrupted` (`Stopped`), `failed` (`Failed`) and `disconnected` (`Offline`) (cross-phase decision 55). Idle gives 8 blank cells.
  - elapsed, 7 cells, right-aligned. `formatElapsed` of the time since `thread.turnStartedAt`, read from `m.conn.clock()`. Shown while a turn runs, otherwise blank. A value above 99 h 59 m shows `99h 59m`, so the field never overflows.
  - calls, 7 cells: `<n> run` while `n > 0`, where `n` counts the running items, nested calls and subagents included (Step 18). A count above 99 shows `99+ run`, the widest value. Otherwise blank.
  - plan, 12 cells: `<done>/<total> plan` while a plan is set (Step 18). Each number above 99 shows `99+`, so the widest value `99+/99+ plan` takes 12 cells. Otherwise blank.
  - queued, 10 cells: `<n> queued` while `len(m.queue) > 0` (Step 32). A count above 99 shows `99+ queued`, the widest value. Otherwise blank.
  - free text, the rest of the row, cut with `fit`. It shows the hint when `hint.text` is set, else the notice when one is set, else the activity while a turn runs, else nothing. A notice stays until the next key press or the next notice, so a stale notice cannot hide the activity for a whole turn.
  The fixed fields take 46 cells (2 + 8 + 7 + 7 + 12 + 10). When the width minus one is below 46, the fields drop whole in this order until the rest fit: queued (leaves 36 cells), plan (leaves 24), calls (leaves 17). At 40 columns only queued drops, so the free text has 3 cells. If the row still does not fit, it is cut with `fit` at the width minus one.
