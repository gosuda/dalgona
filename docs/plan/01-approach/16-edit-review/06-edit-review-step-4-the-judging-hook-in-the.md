Behaviour:
- Scoring: `scoreAnswers` reads every asked question. A missing or out-of-range answer fails its part. The decide adapters fail the whole request on a missing answer (`convertAnswers` in `internal/decide/decisions.go`, `parseLLMAnswers` in `internal/decide/llm.go`), so in production the part fails with the adapter's text. The text `answer <id> is missing or out of range` comes from `scoreAnswers`, which sees an answer map that lacks an id, as the test stubs do. The decide package gains only `RequestSize` (see the `internal/decide/json.go` entry below). Scoring is unchanged (decision 21 of this workstream). Each returned score is read on its own.
- Question selection, edit unit: `sound` is always asked, in every part, when the unit passes the pre-filter, because the pre-filter requires an added line in a file that is not `large`, and `sound` covers every kind. Each other edit question is asked when a file of one of its kinds adds lines (`added > 0`), its `concurrent` condition holds for that file (computed from all added lines of the file), and its `needsTask` condition holds.
- Question selection, turn unit: `sound` and `incomplete`, as in `turnQuestionSet`.
- Verdict, per part: `flagged` when a question other than `sound` scores `reviewFlagThreshold` or more. `uncertain` when none flags and a question other than `sound` scores from `reviewUncertainThreshold` to below `reviewFlagThreshold`. `clean` otherwise. `error` on a failed request.
- Two keys: a flag is spoken only when the `sound` score of the same part is present and below `reviewSoundThreshold` (0.5). A flag with `sound` at 0.5 or more keeps the verdict `flagged` and shows in the clients, but is not spoken. A missing `sound` answer means no flag is spoken.
- Speakers: the flags of `reviewBuiltInSpeakers`, or the flags of `EditFeedback` when that is not nil. A flag that is not a speaker is shown, not spoken.
- `spoken` records the speaking flags in question order. The field is absent when empty. `joinReviews` clears it when the per-turn cap stops the block.
- Parts: the edit unit sends each part as one request, and a unit with one part sends one request. The parts share the unit's one 8 s limit. `parts` in `tool.judged` is the number of requests sent, or 1 when none was. The verdict is the worst part's verdict, the scores are the highest per question (the lowest for `sound`), and the flags and spoken questions are unions over the parts. Each part speaks only on its own `sound` score. A failed part adds no scores. The unit is `error` only when every part fails, and its text then has the part prefix. `truncated` is true when a hunk was omitted, a part failed, or a file was left out as `large`.
- Unchanged: the pre-filter, masking, the background start, the collection at the end of the round, the 8 s limit, the limit of three feedback blocks per turn, and the recovery rules of this step.
